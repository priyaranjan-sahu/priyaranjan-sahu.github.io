## About Me

I'm **Priya Ranjan Sahu**, a Multi-Cloud DevOps & Security Engineer based in India. I design, automate, harden, and optimize infrastructure across **AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud**.

My work sits at the intersection of **Cloud Architecture**, **DevOps/SRE**, **Cloud Security**, and **FinOps** — building production-grade automation that makes cloud environments *safer, cheaper, and self-operating*.

---

## 🎓 Philosophy

> **"One cloud is a choice. Three clouds is a strategy."**

I believe the best engineers don't marry a single cloud provider — they master the patterns that transcend them. My tooling reflects this: every script, module, and platform I build is designed to work identically across AWS, Azure, and GCP.

---

## 🛡️ Security-First by Default

- **Read-only auditing** — all my security tooling operates in read-only mode, safe for production
- **Least privilege** — every module documents minimum granular permissions required
- **Well-Architected alignment** — AWS Well-Architected, Azure Security Benchmark, GCP security best practices
- **Compliance-ready** — CIS Benchmarks, NIST, SOC 2 mappings built into audit outputs

---

## 💰 FinOps-Minded Engineering

Cloud cost isn't an afterthought — it's a first-class design constraint. I build:

- **FOCUS 1.0 normalized spend data** across all clouds
- **Anomaly detection** that catches spend spikes before the bill arrives
- **Forecasting** with confidence intervals for budget planning
- **Rightsizing automation** across 5 waste vectors with ROI calculations
