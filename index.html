01. Core Architectural Tenets

Platform as a Product

I treat infrastructure as an internal product. By abstracting complexity into self-service, GitOps-driven pipelines, I drastically reduce cognitive load for engineering teams and accelerate feature delivery from weeks to hours.

FinOps-Driven Scale

Architecting for scale isn't just about handling traffic; it's about unit economics. I build auto-scaling, spot-instance orchestrated environments that drive down cost-per-compute while maintaining strict SLAs.

Zero-Trust & Policy as Code

Security cannot be an afterthought. I enforce strict OPA/Gatekeeper policies, CIS benchmarks, and identity-aware proxies directly at the infrastructure layer to guarantee compliance by default.

03. Enterprise Impact & Case Studies

LARGE-SCALE TRANSFORMATION

Global Hybrid-Cloud Migration & FinOps Optimization

> CONTEXT_&_PROBLEM

Legacy on-premise data warehouses and monolithic applications were causing severe deployment bottlenecks, dropping critical SLAs, and generating excessive unoptimized annual infrastructure spend.

> ARCHITECTURAL_SOLUTION

Designed a resilient hybrid topology bridging on-premise mainframes with GCP BigQuery and AKS/EKS containerized microservices. Implemented modular Terraform IaC and ArgoCD for immutable, zero-touch GitOps deployments across multiple global regions.

> BUSINESS_IMPACT

  • Achieved 99.99% availability during peak traffic by eliminating single points of failure.
  • Reduced Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR) by 60% through automated self-healing.
  • Shrank recurring cloud spend by 30%+ via FinOps lifecycle management (Rightsizing, Spot-orchestration, and committed use discounts).
LEADERSHIP & OBSERVABILITY

Establishing a Multi-Cloud SRE Center of Excellence

> CONTEXT_&_PROBLEM

Engineering teams spanning multiple product lines were operating in silos with fragmented monitoring, leading to alert fatigue, shadow IT, and missed Service Level Objectives (SLOs).

> ARCHITECTURAL_SOLUTION

Architected and deployed a centralized observability stack (OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Datadog) spanning AWS, Azure, and GCP. Drafted cross-organization SLIs/SLOs, established strict error budget policies, and automated incident response runbooks.

> BUSINESS_IMPACT

  • Unified telemetry across 3 continents, providing C-level executives with real-time operational dashboards.
  • Reduced P1 incident resolution time from hours to under 15 minutes.
  • Fostered a culture of engineering excellence by directly mentoring and upskilling 50+ engineers and 10+ managers.
